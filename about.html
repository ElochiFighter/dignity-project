About Dignity Cycle

Where Dignity Becomes Action.

Dignity Cycle began with a simple idea: no one should have to sacrifice their dignity because they cannot afford menstrual products.

What started as a project focused on period poverty has grown into a community initiative centered on something much bigger: access, education, mental well-being, and dignity.

I created Dignity Cycle to help address the barriers that people experiencing financial hardship and housing insecurity can face when trying to access basic menstrual care. Through fundraising, product donations, education, and community partnerships, the goal is to connect people with the resources they need while creating space for conversations about an issue that is too often overlooked.

Why Period Poverty Matters

Menstrual products are necessities, but they are not always treated that way.

When someone cannot reliably access pads, tampons, or other menstrual products, the effects can reach far beyond their period. It can create barriers to school, work, social activities, and everyday life. It can also contribute to embarrassment, stress, and feelings of isolation.

Dignity Cycle approaches period poverty as both an access issue and a dignity issue.

Everyone deserves to be able to manage their period safely and confidently without having to choose between menstrual care and another basic necessity.
